VAT Rate Sources and Accuracy Note

Last updated: 2026-05-09

EU Calculations uses published EU VAT rate levels as a reference and keeps category calculators as broad guidance. EU countries set their own VAT rates and product category treatment.

Official sources to verify

  • European Commission VAT Rates
  • Your Europe VAT Rules and Rates
  • Taxes in Europe Database
  • Official VIES VAT Number Validation

Why broad categories still need verification

Categories such as food, medicines, accommodation, passenger transport, digital goods, mixed supplies and catering can contain exceptions. The exact VAT rate may depend on product description, service context, local law, region or exemption rules.
