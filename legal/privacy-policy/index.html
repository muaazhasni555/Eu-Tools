Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-09

EU Calculations is designed to work without requiring user accounts. Calculator inputs are processed in the browser where possible and are not saved by the website.

Information we may collect

If you contact us by email or through the contact form, we may receive your name, email address, subject and message. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this policy should be updated with the exact provider names and purposes.

Calculator data

Values entered into calculators are used to display results in the browser. They are not intended to be stored in a database.

Third-party links

Some pages link to official EU or tax authority resources. Those websites have their own privacy practices.
