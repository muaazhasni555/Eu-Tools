About Us

EU Calculations is a free website for European VAT, invoice, payment and business calculators.

Our purpose

EU Calculations helps people quickly estimate common European business figures without spreadsheets. The website focuses on simple, fast and accessible calculators for VAT by category, invoices, VAT numbers, IBAN checks, import costs, GDPR cookie text and business pricing.

Who can use EU Calculations?

The tools are useful for freelancers, ecommerce sellers, small business owners, accountants, agencies, students and professionals who need quick browser-based estimates.

Accuracy approach

EU Calculations keeps calculations simple and transparent. For VAT category rules, we provide broad guidance and point users to official verification sources before making legal, tax or filing decisions.
